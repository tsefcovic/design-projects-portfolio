# A8 – Gears

## Objective
Design and put into cad a gearbox that can be assembled and disassembled.

## Analyze

I then solved for the shafts to see if what we were told to use was strong enough for the application. I then solved the journal bearings and made a sketch of what I wanted the box to look like.
